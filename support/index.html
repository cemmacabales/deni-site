Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to deni.app.help@gmail.com.

Back up and restore

Settings → Your data → Export a backup makes one file of everything in Deni. Save it to Files, or send it to yourself. To bring it back, use Settings → Your data → Restore from a backup, or Restore on Deni's welcome screen. A restore replaces everything on that iPhone with what's in the file.

Moving to a new iPhone

Export a backup on the old iPhone and save it somewhere you can reach from the new one, such as Files or Mail. Install Deni on the new iPhone and tap Restore on its welcome screen. Then turn reminders on in Settings → Reminders.

Reminders aren't arriving

  • In Deni, open Settings → Reminders and check the switch is on. Send yourself the test reminder there.
  • If iOS says notifications are off, tap Open Settings and allow them for Deni.
  • Focus modes and Scheduled Summary can hold reminders back. Allow Deni in the Focus you use.

Delete everything

Settings → Your data → Delete all erases everything Deni has saved, at once, and can't be undone. Export a backup first if you might want it back.

Medical questions

Deni helps you keep track of your medications. It doesn't give medical advice. Check with your doctor or pharmacist before you change how you take a medication.
